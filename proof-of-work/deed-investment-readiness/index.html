THE CORE INSIGHT

Fix the experience around the requirement.

ONE NARROW, TESTABLE BET
OBSERVED / 01

A high-trust request arrives early.

The application asks for identity and address evidence before users can access full property details. For a first-time global investor, the trust cost is high even if the requirement is legitimate.

Personal observation + Deed help guide
BENCHMARK / 02

Another regulated flow does it differently.

SmartCrowd describes 30 days of limited access after registration, while still requiring completed verification before investing or accessing certain documents. Deed’s allowable boundary needs its own compliance review.

Competitor process, not legal permission
SOLUTION / 03

Make verification legible.

Introduce a policy-filtered Explorer, document readiness checklist, reason-coded exceptions, and clear case tracking. Measure movement through the entire funnel, not merely KYC starts.

Hypothesis to validate through an A/B test
PRODUCT RECOMMENDATION

Build A + one thin slice of B.

Explorer access + KYC preflight, supported by structured verification status and recovery. Start with education and readiness; defer restricted property disclosure until Compliance explicitly approves fields.

    DRAFT FOR COMPLIANCE REVIEW
    01 / PROBLEM

    The next step costs more trust than it needs to.

    Deed's public materials indicate that completed KYC is required to access full property details. This was also encountered during an independent prospective-user attempt. The concern is not the legitimacy of verification. It is the absence of a clearly separated learning, preparation, and recovery path around verification.

    How might we help unverified prospects become informed and document-ready before entering mandatory KYC, while preserving access controls and regulator-approved communications?

    Confidence: the present KYC gate and document rules are documented; funnel abandonment magnitude, conversion uplift and internal rejection distributions are unknown.

    02 / USERS

    Who this serves

    PRIMARY

    Global first-time explorer

    Wants to evaluate whether Deed is worth trusting before sharing a passport and proof of address.

    SECONDARY

    Investor needing correction

    Has made the effort to submit documents and now needs a specific next step without repeated support messages.

    03 / GOAL

    One measurable outcome

    Primary: increase the share of eligible registered users who complete compliant KYC and reach a first verified opportunity view. Leading measures: preflight completion, KYC start-to-submit, first-pass approval and recoveries completed. Guardrails: restricted-data leakage = zero; compliance escalations do not worsen; support contacts per case do not increase.

    04 / SCOPE

    Deliberately small MVP

    IN SCOPE / P0
    • Policy-controlled educational Explorer (no live deal data by default)
    • Passport, POA and UAE-resident preflight
    • Structured case status and safe remediation guidance
    • Authenticated backend status read contract
    • Funnel events, non-PII reason codes, audit trail
    OUT OF SCOPE
    • Removing or auto-approving KYC
    • Real investor funding or property transaction flow
    • Live offer/valuation details before compliance review
    • New identity verification vendor
    • AML rule explanations, fraud scoring, document OCR
    05 / DEPENDENCIES

    Decisions that cannot be guessed

    Compliance: exact permitted pre-KYC content and disclosures by user classification; wording and legal basis for restricted categories. CTO/Engineering: current identity state mapping, vendor webhooks and API boundaries. Growth: acquisition mix and funnel baseline. Support: common case failure reasons and escalation routes. Design: accessibility and native parity.

    Hard stop: Do not ship the Explorer or expose live investment content until Compliance signs off the eligibility and disclosure matrix.

    03. Engineering constraints

    ACCESS CONTROL

    Only approved disclosure fields served to unverified users. No live property financial payloads returned then hidden client-side.

    EVENT INTEGRITY

    Webhook signature validation, monotonic case revision, idempotency keys, deduplication and dead-letter/reconciliation strategy.

    PRIVACY

    No raw ID documents or personal addresses in analytics, logs, notifications or the prototype. Signed short-lived access in authorized uploader.

    OBSERVABILITY

    Transition latency, provider timeout/retries, rejected actions, notification delivery, accessibility and safe support handoff.

    01 / EXPERIMENT

    Do progressive education + preflight actually help?

    Hypothesis: a control group using the current documented journey will have lower eligible-user verified opportunity views than a treatment offering policy-approved Explorer education and preflight guidance.

    Assignment: eligible new accounts, consistent assignment across app/web, stratify by UAE status and acquisition source. Analysis: intention-to-treat; track exposure, KYC completion, first-pass review quality and support cost. Proposed success criterion: statistically credible improvement in primary outcome with no deterioration in compliance guardrails. Actual thresholds must be chosen from a pre-experiment power analysis, not fabricated.

    02 / DELIVERY

    Two-sprint illustrative rollout

    SPRINT 01 / FOUNDATION

    Policy, preflight, contract

    Stakeholder workshop; disclosure matrix; content and design; validation rules; preflight UI; case-state API skeleton; schema and audit telemetry; feature flags and unit tests.

    SPRINT 02 / EXPERIENCE

    Status, recovery, measurement

    Native/web status screens; reason-code translations; secure handoff; event taxonomy; end-to-end tests; experiment controls; support playbook and analytics.

    Dependency-first plan: if Compliance cannot approve an Explorer disclosure matrix, ship document readiness + status clarity first. No public deal previews.

    03 / RELEASE

    Non-negotiable acceptance gates

    01 Compliance signs exact content access rules, disclaimer copy and security/privacy treatment.
    02 Negative tests prove unauthorized identities receive no property-specific restricted payloads.
    03 Passport/POA boundary and exception test cases pass on web, iOS and Android.
    04 Retry/idempotency, provider outage and contradictory-webhook reconciliation pass integration tests.
    05 Accessibility checks, content QA, analytics validation and customer-support training complete.
    06 Flag-controlled ramp with explicit stop/rollback owner; experiment reviewed by Growth, Compliance and Engineering.
